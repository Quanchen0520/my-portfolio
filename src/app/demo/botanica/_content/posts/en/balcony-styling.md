Many people think their balcony is too small or too sunny to style. In truth, with **layered heights** and **matching materials**, even the smallest balcony becomes a charming green corner.

## Build height in layers

Group plants into three heights:

- Floor: large plants or tall pots
- Middle: medium pots on shelves or stools
- Above eye level: hanging plants, like string of pearls

The moment you spread out the heights, the space gains depth.

## The warmth of materials

Clay, rattan, wood, cotton and linen flatter greenery best. Avoid too many colours at once — let the plants be the lead.

> Empty space is the most underrated styling tool.

## A chair you can actually sit on

Do not forget a place for yourself. A small chair, a cup of tea — only then does a balcony truly become part of your life rather than a display.
