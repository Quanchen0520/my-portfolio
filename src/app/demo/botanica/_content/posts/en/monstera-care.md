The Monstera is almost everyone's first plant. It is forgiving, shade-tolerant, and those split leaves are endlessly satisfying. But "easy" does not mean "no care" — a few principles will keep it lush and happy.

## Watering: dry is safer than wet

A Monstera fears **root rot** far more than thirst. The simplest test: push a finger two knuckles deep into the soil. If it feels dry, water it.

- Spring & summer growth: every 5–7 days
- Autumn & winter rest: every 10–14 days

## Light: bright but indirect

It loves bright light without direct sun. Near a window, behind a sheer curtain, is ideal.

> Leaves that refuse to split are usually a sign of too little light.

## Repotting and cleaning

Repot once each spring to give the roots more room. Wipe the leaves with a damp cloth now and then — it removes dust and helps you spot pests early.

Caring for a plant was never an exact science. It is simply the slow act of getting to know it.
