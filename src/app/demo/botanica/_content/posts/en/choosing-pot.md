When choosing pots, people often look only at the shape and forget it directly affects the health of the roots.

## Drainage first

However beautiful it is, **be wary of any pot without a drainage hole**. If you fall for one, slip the plant in its plastic nursery pot inside, so you can lift it out to drain.

## Choosing a material

- Terracotta: breathable, great drainage, suits most plants
- Glazed: holds water, good for moisture-loving species
- Concrete: weighty and calm, ideal for large plants

## Getting the size right

Go just one size up from the current pot. Jumping too large keeps the soil too wet and can harm the roots.

> A good pot makes both the plant and the room look better.

Choose slowly, repot slowly, and you will find the one that fits you both.
