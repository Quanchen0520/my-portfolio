We always say we have no time. But "slow" does not require much time — only a little intention.

## Start with a glass of water

The first thing in the morning is not your phone, but a little water for your plants. Crouch, observe, touch the soil — this simple act connects a still-sleepy you to the present.

## Read today's leaves

- Any new shoots appearing?
- Are the tips turning yellow?
- Which pot needs to turn toward the sun?

Put your attention on these small changes, and anxiety finds a little less room.

> Caring for plants is really practising caring for yourself.

Ten minutes later, you will walk into the day with a steadier heart.
